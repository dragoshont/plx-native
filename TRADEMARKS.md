label and an original geometric screen icon generated by
`tools/tizen/build-icon.py`. This does not choose a permanent commercial brand
or assert trademark clearance. The icon's source and output are offered under
the repository's GPL-3.0-or-later terms.

project's active branding. The original reservation and description remain at

`PlxNative0` / `PlxNative0.Plx` identifiers remain for release-link and installed
app compatibility, not as a new branding claim. Published releases still show
their original display identity and are not replaced by this source migration.
